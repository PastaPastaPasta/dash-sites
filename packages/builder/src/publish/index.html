Publish your own

A website under your Dash name, stored on Dash Platform and open to anyone at yourname.dash.site. You need a Dash identity with a little credit. Everything happens in your browser.

  1. Pick a name

    Names are 3 to 63 letters, digits and hyphens. Type one and this page asks Dash Platform whether it is free, checking the proof itself.

  2. Know the cost

    Storage is paid once, when you publish, at about 0.3 DASH per megabyte. Text and code are compressed first.

  3. Sign in and publish

    Write a page in the editor, or upload a folder or zip of a site you already have. You see the exact fee before anything is sent.

    Sign in with DashPay

    Your keys stay in the wallet. The page gets a key that can only publish sites, with a spending limit and an end date you set when you approve it.

    Sign in with a private key instead

    Use an identity ID or name and one of its authentication keys, or an identity file. The key stays in the page's memory. It is never stored, logged or sent anywhere.

    Continue with a private key

    Prefer a terminal? The same publisher is a command:

    dash-sites publish ./my-site --name alice --network mainnet
